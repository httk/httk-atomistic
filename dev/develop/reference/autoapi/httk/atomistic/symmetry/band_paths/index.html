httk.atomistic.symmetry.band_paths¶

Exact band-structure k-paths in the HPKOT convention.

The convention is that of Hinuma, Pizzi, Kumagai, Oba and Tanaka, “Band structure diagram paths based on crystallography”, Comput. Mater. Sci. 128, 140 (2017), as implemented by SeeK-path 2.2.2, whose tables are vendored in _band_path_data. The path is chosen from the IT standard conventional cell after the lattice choices that the ITA symbol leaves free (orthorhombic axis order, the monoclinic reduced cell, the triclinic reduced reciprocal cell), and every selection rule and k-point coordinate is evaluated exactly from the rational metric tensor.

Module Contents¶

class httk.atomistic.symmetry.band_paths.BandPathResult[source]¶

Store a recommended band-structure path in the HPKOT convention.

points are fractional coordinates in the reciprocal basis of structure (the HPKOT primitive cell), keyed by SeeK-path’s ASCII labels (GAMMA, X_1, primes as X') in table order. path lists the segments in order. transform is the integer row-convention matrix with basis(structure) = transform * basis(primitive), where primitive is primitive_cell() of the input; its determinant is +1, or -1 when the conventional basis was left-handed (the result basis is always right-handed).

Parameters:

  • structure – The HPKOT primitive cell the coordinates refer to.

  • spacegroup – The IT standard setting of the crystal’s space group.

  • bravais_lattice – The Bravais lattice symbol, such as "cF".

  • bravais_lattice_extended – The extended HPKOT symbol, such as "cF2".

  • points – A read-only mapping from label to exact fractional reciprocal coordinates.

  • path – The ordered (start, end) label segments.

  • transform – The integer row-convention basis change from the primitive cell.

httk.atomistic.symmetry.band_paths.band_path(structure, *, time_reversal=True, tolerance=None, limit_denominator=None)[source]¶

Return the HPKOT band-structure path of a crystal, with exact k-point coordinates.

The space group is the one the structure holds: the declared group of an existing ASU (used as stored, without spglib), or the recognized group of a raw structure. The input is brought to its IT standard conventional cell by primitive_cell(); the lattice choices the setting leaves free are then made as SeeK-path does (orthorhombic axes ordered by length among symmetry-allowed permutations, the monoclinic Parthé–Gelato reduced cell, the triclinic Niggli-reduced reciprocal cell), and the extended Bravais symbol, parameters and points are evaluated exactly from the rational metric. Exact extended-symbol ties are resolved as SeeK-path resolves them; equal-length axis-order ties and near ties within spglib’s tolerance may give an equivalent, differently oriented cell.

With time_reversal=False and a group without inversion, every non-GAMMA point X gains a partner X' at -X and the path is repeated with primed endpoints.

Parameters:

  • structure (httk.atomistic.models.structure.like.StructureLike) – The fully periodic crystal.

  • time_reversal (bool) – Whether time-reversal symmetry relates k and -k.

  • tolerance (float | None) – The Cartesian recognition tolerance, or None to derive it.

  • limit_denominator (int | None) – The maximum denominator for idealised free parameters, or None to retain their exact stated values.

Returns:

The HPKOT primitive cell, symbols, points and path.

Raises:

  • ImportError – If recognition is needed and the optional spglib dependency is unavailable.

  • ValueError – If the structure has site moments, its conventional metric is not rational, or primitive_cell() refuses it.

Return type:

BandPathResult
